HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, none of them typing.

Expense Tracker sits quietly in the background and does the bookkeeping your bank already gave you the data for.

01

Grant read access to one folder

Expense Tracker only reads messages from senders your bank actually uses — nothing else on your phone is touched.

02

Each alert gets parsed

Amount, merchant, account, and direction — debit or credit — are pulled out the moment the SMS arrives.

03

Your ledger builds itself

Entries land in a running total under a category, across every account you've connected, automatically.

FEATURES

Built around how bank SMS actually reads.

MULTI-BANK

Works across every account you carry

Meezan, HBL, UBL, Easypaisa, JazzCash — Expense Tracker recognizes each bank's own alert format and merges them into one ledger, so a payment from any account lands in the same place.

ON-DEVICE

Nothing leaves your phone

Parsing happens locally. Your balances and transaction history are never uploaded anywhere.

LEARNS

Corrects itself once you correct it

Re-tag a merchant once — "Careem" as Transport, not Food — and every future alert from it follows suit.

BREAKDOWN

A monthly total you can actually trust

See spend by category and by account, side by side, without exporting anything to a sheet first.

ABOUT

Why we built Expense Tracker

Every bank in Pakistan already sends a text for every rupee that moves. We kept retyping those same numbers into a spreadsheet at the end of each month — once for groceries, once for the electricity bill, once for whatever Careem had quietly added up to.

Expense Tracker exists so that step disappears. The bank has already done the work of recording the transaction; Expense Tracker's only job is to read what it sent and keep the total honest.
